ExertionalPrivacy policy

What the app keeps, and where

Last updated on 17 November 2026. This policy covers the Exertional app for iPhone.

Summary

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. Exertional has no account, no server of its own and no ads. Its developer never sees your hearings, your clients or your notes.

Hearing files and limits

Saved jobs, the limits of a hypothetical, hearing files and notes are stored only in the app's folder on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.

A PDF or text you export goes only where you send it from the iOS share sheet. Do not put a claimant's name in a hearing file unless you are allowed to carry it on your phone.

Reference data

The occupations, ratings and rules ship inside the app. Looking something up makes no network request.

Purchases and usage

Purchases are handled by Apple. To keep a record of them, Exertional uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase history of the app. See RevenueCat's policy.

With it, the app sends a few anonymous counters: how many times the Pro screen was shown and closed without buying, which screen it was opened from, and in which version of the app the rating prompt was shown. These numbers are used only to understand and improve how the app is offered. They carry no content from the app, no name, email or other identifier, and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. Nothing is sent in test builds.

What is not in the app

No advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, no access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Exertional is a professional reference. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.
