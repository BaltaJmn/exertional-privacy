The DOT, on the table, while the vocational expert is still talking

For representatives, attorneys and vocational experts at Social Security disability hearings.

1. Look up

All 12,741 occupations of the Dictionary of Occupational Titles, fourth edition, revised 1991. Search by title, by part of a word, by task or by code, or paste several codes at once. Works with no signal in the hearing room.

2. Check the hypothetical

Enter the limits of the judge's hypothetical once. Each job the expert cites is checked against them, factor by factor. FITS or the exact requirement that exceeds the limit.

3. Grid rules

The Medical-Vocational Guidelines of 20 CFR Part 404, Subpart P, Appendix 2: pick capacity, age, education and past work and read the rule and its decision in the regulation's own words.

4. Hearing files

Keep the cited jobs, the limits and your notes for each hearing, and export them as a PDF for the brief.

Not legal advice

Exertional is a reference. It is not affiliated with the Social Security Administration or the Department of Labor, and it does not decide a claim.
